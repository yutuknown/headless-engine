# Search: quantum computing breakthrough 2025

## ✨ AI Overview
The year 2025 marked a massive turning point for quantum computing as practical error correction, record-breaking qubit counts, and high gate-fidelities pushed systems out of pure physics labs and into early enterprise deployment. Major milestones included hardware scaling, new architectural chip designs, and significant commercial and government investments.

### 🏛️ Major Hardware & Scaling Milestones
- **Record Neutral-Atom Arrays**: Caltech researchers demonstrated a massive array trapping 6,100 cesium atoms using a split laser grid, keeping qubits in superposition for a record 13 seconds while moving atoms across the array.
- **Topological and Cat Qubits**: Microsoft debuted its Majorana 1 topological quantum processor concept, while Alice & Bob achieved over an hour of qubit coherence using error-resistant cat qubits.
- **Four-Nines Gate Fidelity**: IonQ crossed the 'four-nines' benchmark, achieving a world-class 99.99% two-qubit gate performance alongside its Oxford Ionics team.
- **Commercial Systems**: Quantinuum launched Helios, touting record-level accuracy for enterprise clients.
- **Certified Randomness**: JPMorgan Chase and Quantinuum successfully generated over 71,000 certified random bits, providing a practical cryptographic advantage verified by classical high-performance computing.
